Wanted: ring and structural analyses

Ring composition, where a surah's opening and closing mirror each other around a centre, is documented in the scholarly literature. This site names the phenomenon and asserts no ring outline for any surah, because a real one has to be transcribed from a published, page-cited analysis, never computed. Inventing verse ranges under a scholar's name would misattribute work they did not do, so the count below is zero and stays zero until someone brings a citation.

Claiming one means naming the scholar, the volume and the page, and giving the verse ranges exactly as published. Cuypers, Farrin● and Islahi● are the obvious starting points. The Patterns page carries the one narrow mechanical proxy the site did test corpus-wide, and its null result, which says nothing either way about that literature.
