Frequency and distribution insights from the corpus. Each card ends with its verification status.

Note on counting. Word counts in the Qur'an depend on whether one counts root occurrences, surface forms, definite vs indefinite, singular vs plural. Where a popular figure depends on a specific counting rule, this is labeled Nuanced with the counting method named.

Earth and sea

Root a-r-ḍ (earth, land): 461. Root b-ḥ-r (sea): 42.

Verified root counts from the Leeds Quranic Arabic Corpus (Dukes 2009–2017). A widely cited surface-form count (13 "land" / 32 "sea") uses specific noun senses only; see counting note above. Nuanced on the popular interpretation.

Day, month, year

Day (y-w-m, root): 405 occurrences (Leeds corpus); broader counts including compound forms such as yawma-idhin reach 475. Singular form yawm: 365 by one widely cited counting. Month (sh-h-r, root): 21. Singular shahr: 12 by common counting method. Year: not a single canonical word in the Qur'an.

Verified root counts from Leeds. Nuanced for y-w-m: the 405/475 difference turns on whether compound forms are included; both are defensible. See Roots for detail.

Surah length

Mean: 54.6 verses. Median: 30. Longest: al-Baqarah (286). Shortest: al-Kawthar, al-'Asr, al-Nasr (3 each).

Verified from Tanzil (114 surahs, 6,236 verses by Cairo numbering).

Hapax legomena

12,004 surface forms (out of 18,993 unique forms) occur exactly once. 395 roots (out of 1,642) occur exactly once.

Verified from Leeds corpus word-form and root counts.

Roots unique to one period

547 roots appear only in Meccan surahs (zero Medinan occurrences). 198 roots appear only in Medinan surahs.

Verified from Leeds + Cairo 1924 four-period classification. Nuanced: a handful of surahs have mixed-period classification in some traditions; the boundary roots may shift by a few in other schemes.

Fawatih (opening particles)

29 surahs open with disconnected letters (al-muqatta'at). 14 unique letter combinations across these 29 surahs.

Verified from Tanzil text. Nuanced on interpretation; meaning is discussed across the tafsir tradition.
