How to read this

The distance is the square root of the Jensen-Shannon divergence between two surahs' root counts, from 0 (same proportions) to 1 (no root in common). ● Short surahs look distant from everything by arithmetic alone, so each distance is compared with what pooling both surahs' verses and reassigning them at random would give (2,000 draws), and the excess over that null is what the colors show. A large excess means different vocabularies, not different themes or styles; the null keeps verse counts, not token counts, so pairs whose verses differ in length are calibrated less well. With 2,000 draws the smallest possible p-value is 1 in 2,001, and 3,703 of the 6,441 pairs reach it, so p and q cannot rank those pairs against each other. The lists above rank by excess over the null, which is a size of difference, not a strength of evidence. ● The full table, with p and q values, is on Export as surah-distance.
