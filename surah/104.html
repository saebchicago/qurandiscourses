Surah al-Humazah الهمزة

al-Humazah is surah 104 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 9 verses and 33 word-units, is classified Meccan, and is 32nd in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
