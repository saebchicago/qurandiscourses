Surah al-'Asr العصر

al-'Asr is surah 103 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 3 verses and 14 word-units, is classified Meccan, and is 13th in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
