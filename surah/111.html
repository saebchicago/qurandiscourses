Surah al-Masad المسد

al-Masad is surah 111 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 5 verses and 23 word-units, is classified Meccan, and is 6th in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
