Surah al-Qari'ah القارعة

al-Qari'ah is surah 101 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 11 verses and 36 word-units, is classified Meccan, and is 30th in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
