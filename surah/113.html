Surah al-Falaq الفلق

al-Falaq is surah 113 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 5 verses and 23 word-units, is classified Meccan, and is 20th in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
