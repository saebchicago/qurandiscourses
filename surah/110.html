Surah al-Nasr النصر

al-Nasr is surah 110 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 3 verses and 19 word-units, is classified Medinan, and is 114th in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
