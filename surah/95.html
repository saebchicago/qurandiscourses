Surah al-Tin التين

al-Tin is surah 95 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 8 verses and 34 word-units, is classified Meccan, and is 28th in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
