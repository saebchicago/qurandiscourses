Surah al-Qadr القدر

al-Qadr is surah 97 of the Qur'an's 114. It has 5 verses and 30 word-units, is classified Meccan, and is 25th in the Cairo 1924 revelation order. With Mahmoud al-Husary, its recitation lasts about 1 min.
