Themes

Gateways from a life theme to the places in the Qur'an where its vocabulary works hardest. Pick a theme; get its root families, the computed key passages, and a self-study kit.

Depth:
Keys 1 2 3

~ Themes are gateways from an editorially chosen root family to the passages where that vocabulary clusters, so a theme can also live in verses using none of these words. How themes are built.

Self-study kit (works for any theme)
  1. Open each passage and read the whole discourse around it (use Prev/Next on the Read page): who is being addressed, and what came before?
  2. Follow one root into the Roots explorer: where else does it cluster, and in which period?
  3. Write down, in your own words, what the passages agree on and where they differ.

Forgiveness

Root families: غ ف ر gh-f-r (to forgive, cover over), ع ف و ʿ-f-w (to pardon, efface), ت و ب t-w-b (to turn back, repent), ص ف ح ṣ-f-ḥ (to overlook, forgive).

Densest in: al-Nasr, al-Tahrim, al-Munafiqun.

Key passages: 24:22, 64:14, 4:64.

Marriage and spouses

Root families: ز و ج z-w-j (spouse, pair), ن ك ح n-k-ḥ (to marry), ط ل ق ṭ-l-q (to divorce, release).

Densest in: al-Tahrim, al-Ahzab, al-Mursalat.

Key passages: 2:230, 2:232, 33:50.

Children and family

Root families: و ل د w-l-d (to give birth; child), ب ن ي b-n-y (to build; son, children), ي ت م y-t-m (orphan), ذ ر ر dh-r-r (offspring, progeny).

Densest in: al-Ikhlas, al-Zalzalah, al-Duha.

Key passages: 2:83, 2:215, 4:36.

Trade and wealth

Densest in: al-Humazah, al-Qari'ah, al-Layl.

Key passages: 6:152, 2:275, 30:39.
