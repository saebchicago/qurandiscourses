Themes

Gateways from a life theme to the places in the Qur'an where its vocabulary works hardest. Pick a theme; get its root families, the computed key passages, and a self-study kit.

Depth:
Keys 1 2 3

~ Themes are gateways from an editorially chosen root family to the passages where that vocabulary clusters, so a theme can also live in verses using none of these words. How themes are built.

Self-study kit (works for any theme)
  1. Open each passage and read the whole discourse around it (use Prev/Next on the Read page): who is being addressed, and what came before?
  2. Follow one root into the Roots explorer: where else does it cluster, and in which period?
  3. Write down, in your own words, what the passages agree on and where they differ.
