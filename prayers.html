Prayers and remembrance in the text

Where the Qur'an has people say رَبَّنَا (rabbana, "Our Lord") as an address to God, and how often it uses the roots of glorification, praise, remembrance and forgiveness. This page describes what the text contains. It does not say what to recite or when.

Read before using

Whether a verse is a model prayer depends on who is speaking. Several of the verses below are spoken by people the text itself condemns, for instance on the Day of Judgment. This page finds the words; it does not decide who says them. Open each verse in context before using it, and see the limits below.

Glorification, praise, remembrance and forgiveness

Token and verse counts for four roots, with the lemmas that carry them. A root is wider than the act its name suggests, so read the lemmas.

RootTokensVersesLemmas
  • sbH includes senses other than glorification, such as swimming or moving along (21:33).
  • Hmd includes al-Hamid, a name of God, besides the act of praising.
  • *kr includes dhikr as a name for the Qur'an itself, and dhakar, male.
  • gfr includes al-Ghafur and al-Ghaffar, names of God, besides asking forgiveness.

●

How these were found, and what they leave out

  • A token counts when its lemma is rabb, it ends in the first-person plural suffix, and the Uthmani text puts the accusative vowel on the ba. ●
  • Two tokens with that form are not addresses and are left out: 25:21, "or see our Lord" (the object of a verb), and 35:34, "indeed our Lord" (after inna).
  • The forms رَبُّنَا and رَبِّنَا speak about the Lord, not to Him. They occur 18 and 21 times and are not listed here.
  • Prayers in other forms are not found by this search: those that begin with another address, or with an imperative alone. The list is the text's "Our Lord" prayers, not all of its prayers.
  • Speaker, occasion and meaning are not computed. Hadith is not used on this page.

If you know of a verse this search misses or misreads, or can advise on appropriate use, open a correction issue. The table is also on Export as prayers-vocative.
