Changelog

Every correction and material change to Discourses since launch. Logged here as a record of accountability. Site maintainers commit to logging future corrections in the same place.

2026

May

  • Surah length statistics corrected. Mean updated from 54.6 to 54.7 verses per surah. Median updated from 30 to 39 verses. Both errors traced to incorrect aggregation method; recomputed directly against Leeds corpus.
  • y-w-m count reframed as Nuanced. Earlier showed 475; corpus.quran.com lists 405 for the nominal yawm, while broader counts including the compound yawma-idhin reach 475. Both counts now shown with explanation.
  • k-w-n count corrected. 1,361 to 1,390 after triangulation against corpus.quran.com via multiple independent lookups.
  • r-b-b count corrected. 975 to 980 after corpus.quran.com triangulation.
  • b-h-r count corrected. 41 to 42 after Leeds verification.
  • Iltifat attribution expanded. The al-Fatihah 1:5 grammatical shift note now credits classical attestation in al-Suyuti's al-Itqan alongside modern scholarship by Abdel Haleem (BSOAS 1992). Earlier version credited Haleem only.
  • Organization name reviewed. The parent organization uses both "Association of Quranic Understanding" (in its page title and some documents) and "Association for Quranic Understanding" (on its banner and in most published references). Independent obituaries unanimously use "for." The site previously claimed to standardize on "of"; the more honest treatment is to note the variation. References on this site now use "Association for Quranic Understanding," matching the more common external usage.
  • Khan biography expanded. The About page now documents Khan's full academic and interfaith record: Aligarh and Rampur education, UIC doctoral work, Parliament of the World's Religions trusteeship, WCMIR presidency, AQU directorship.
  • Khan's 2013 work added. An Exercise in Understanding the Qur'an (AQU, ISBN 9781567447736) now cited alongside Reflections on the Qur'an. Foreword by Mustansir Mir noted.
  • Leeds Corpus bundled. 6,236 verses, 77,430 words, 1,642 roots now available as static JSON. Word-by-word morphology live on Read at Scholar depth. GPL compliance via NOTICE.md.
  • Site launch. Initial deployment of 13 pages.

Corrections welcome. Open a GitHub issue at github.com/saebchicago/qurandiscourses or by opening a discussion at github.com/saebchicago/qurandiscourses/discussions.
