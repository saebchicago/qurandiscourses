# Licence outreach drafts

Two sources needed a reply from their authors before their data could be
used (see `docs/ELEVATION-PLAN.md`, A3). Both messages below were sent from the
maintainer's address; see Status at the end for the replies. Do not send them
again.

## MASAQ (Mendeley Data page states CC BY-NC 3.0)

Subject: Use of MASAQ in a free, non-commercial Qur'an study site

> Assalamu alaykum. I maintain Divine Discourses (https://divinediscourses.org),
> a free, non-commercial study site and open dataset built on the Leeds
> Quranic Arabic Corpus. We would like to use MASAQ with full attribution. The
> Mendeley page lists CC BY-NC 3.0. Our exported tables are released under the
> GPL, which does not carry a non-commercial restriction, so we would keep
> MASAQ out of those exports unless you are willing to grant broader terms for
> that use. Could you confirm (1) that non-commercial display and analysis on
> the site is welcome, and (2) whether you would permit redistribution of the
> derived tables under GPL? We would credit you as you prefer. Thank you.

## QuranMorph (no data licence named)

Subject: Licence for the QuranMorph data

> Assalamu alaykum. I maintain Divine Discourses (https://divinediscourses.org),
> a free, non-commercial study site. We would like to cite and, if you allow,
> use QuranMorph annotations with attribution, but we could not find a data
> licence. Could you tell us under what terms the data may be used and
> redistributed? Until we hear from you we link to QuranMorph and do not copy
> its data. Thank you.

## Status

- **QuranMorph: answered 2026-10-19.** SinaLab (Diyam Aqraa, copied to Prof. Mustafa Jarrar) wrote: "You can use the data under the CC-BY-4.0 license; thus, you must cite properly. Please attribute this work to SinaLab and put a link the corpus https://sina.birzeit.edu/quran/". Use with that attribution; keep derived QuranMorph tables under their own CC-BY-4.0 notice. The email is in the maintainer's mailbox; it is the only record of the grant, so keep it. Not yet used on the site: nothing is credited until a layer ships.
- **MASAQ: sent 2026-10-20, no reply yet.** The message went to Dr. Sawalha with four co-authors copied; one cc address (an Amazon address listed for one co-author) bounced. Until the authors answer, MASAQ stays out of the exports and off the site.
