# Prayers: who is speaking (reviewer sheet)

`/prayers` lists every place the text has people address God as *rabbana*, and it deliberately does not say who is speaking, because several of the verses are spoken by people the text condemns. This sheet is for a qualified reviewer to fill in the speaker for each verse from the text and a source they cite, so that a labeled column can be added to the page. Nothing here is a finding yet: the Speaker column is empty on purpose, and no label ships until each row has a reviewer and a source.

Suggested values: believers, prophet, angels, condemned, other (say who). Mark any row where the attribution is disputed among scholars and cite both readings.
