Review our work

We invite qualified experts to check our work for accuracy. Every page here states where its facts come from; a reviewer tests those statements against their own expertise.

Who we're looking for

  • Scholars of Qur'anic studies
  • Scholars of classical Arabic
  • Scholars of Islamic intellectual history
  • Scholars of tafsir across traditions
  • Computational linguists working with Arabic

We welcome reviewers from different interpretive traditions. Where scholars differ, we aim to show the range of views rather than settle the question.

What reviewing involves

  • A scoped review of specific pages or datasets that you choose.
  • You flag errors, missing context, or unfair framing.
  • We fix what needs fixing and log each change publicly on the Corrections page.

Our review standard

  1. Scoped review through appropriate professional channels.
  2. Named credit only with the reviewer's explicit consent.
  3. A public corrections record.

Independence

Reviewers assess accuracy. Credit does not imply endorsement by the reviewer or their institution. The site remains independently maintained.
