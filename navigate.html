● Leeds corpus v0.4.

ℹ How this is computed

● verse counts from Tanzil Uthmani text. Meccan/Medinan classification per Cairo 1924 edition.

Method: surah profile figures. Word-unit and root counts are drawn from the Leeds Quranic Arabic Corpus v0.4 morphology files, one token per morphology entry (Leeds' Uthmani tokenization). Chronology period follows the Egyptian Standard (Cairo 1924) Meccan/Medinan and four-period classification. Root diversity ratio divides distinct roots by all word-units in the surah, including function words with no tagged root. These are descriptive corpus counts, not claims about meaning or emphasis.

The 114 surahs at a glance ●

Each dot is a surah: length (word-units, log scale) against lexical variety (distinct roots ÷ word-units), colored by chronological period. Hover a dot for the surah; click to read it. Notice for yourself how the periods separate — and where the exceptions sit.

ℹ How this is computed

Token and distinct-root counts from the bundled Leeds v0.4 morphology (see the method note above); period from the Cairo 1924 convention. Longer texts repeat vocabulary, so diversity falls with length for purely statistical reasons — the pattern is descriptive, not a claim about style or meaning.
